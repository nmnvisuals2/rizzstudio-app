How it works

From playlist to light show in minutes

No cue lists or scene programming. RizzStudio works through your set the way a lighting designer would, at a speed no lighting designer can match.

Open your set

Drop in songs (MP3, WAV, AIFF, M4A, FLAC) or an Ableton .als set. Every song becomes part of the playlist.

It listens

Tempo, key, sections, drops, swing, silences and every kick, clap and hat are detected in under a second per song.

AI programs it

Your local Claude Code or Codex designs the cues: movers, gobos, colour, strobes, lasers, pixels and FX.

Play it live

Run the show straight to your rig over Art-Net, sACN or DMX, or export it into Ableton as DMX-over-MIDI.

Music analysis

It hears the music like a lighting designer

RizzStudio splits each track into percussive and tonal parts and finds every hit, so the lights follow what's actually playing in the track.

  • Kick, clap, hats, percussion, bassline and lead lanes
  • Numbered drops: each one hits harder than the last
  • Pre-drop moments, snare rolls and cuts are caught down to 1/16 notes
  • Swing-aware: strobes and chases follow the groove
  • Optional Demucs stem separation for even cleaner detection
Stage preview

See the whole show before the gig

A realistic 3D stage shows the exact output your rig will receive: moving heads that pan and tilt as the DMX says, beam cones, gobos on the floor, lasers, pixel bars and real flame and CO₂ footage.

  • Club, festival, warehouse, concert and DJ-booth layouts
  • AI stage layout from a sketch or venue photo
  • LED wall fed live from TouchDesigner over Syphon
  • Render the show to an MP4 video (up to 4K / 60 fps)
Ableton → DMX

Ableton becomes your lighting desk

The finished show is baked into Ableton as MIDI clips, so lights stay locked to the music even when you change tempo, jump scenes or mix live. You don't need a separate show computer or timecode.

  • Arrangement show file or Session clips per song
  • Bundled TouchDesigner receiver → Art-Net / sACN
  • Real fixture personalities via the Open Fixture Library
  • Live output on a virtual MIDI port, plus direct Art-Net

FAQ

Questions, answered

What is RizzStudio?

RizzStudio is a macOS app that creates automatic AI light shows from your songs. Drop in audio files or an Ableton Live set: it analyses every kick, clap, drop and silence, programs a complete beat-accurate DMX show, and plays it live over Art-Net, sACN or DMX, or exports it into Ableton. More DAWs are coming.

Which DAWs does RizzStudio support?

RizzStudio works with plain audio files (MP3, WAV, AIFF, M4A, FLAC), so songs from any DAW or DJ library work today. Ableton Live has deep integration: sets open directly and shows export back as DMX-over-MIDI. Integrations with more DAWs are planned.

Is RizzStudio free?

Yes. RizzStudio is currently a free download for macOS from GitHub releases.

Do I need an OpenAI or Anthropic API key?

No. RizzStudio uses the Claude Code or Codex command-line tool you already have installed and signed in. Without either, a built-in rules engine still programs the full show.

Which lights does it support?

Moving heads, RGB washes, strobes, blinders, lasers, pixel bars, LED walls and special effects such as haze, CO₂, sparkulars, confetti and flames. Fixture profiles from the Open Fixture Library map the show to each fixture's real DMX channels.

Do I need Ableton Live?

You can open plain MP3, WAV, AIFF, M4A or FLAC files, preview the show on the 3D stage and render it to video without Ableton. To run real lights from the show, Ableton plays the exported MIDI, or RizzStudio can output Art-Net directly in Perform mode.

Which Macs are supported?

macOS 14 Sonoma or later on Apple Silicon (M1 and newer).
