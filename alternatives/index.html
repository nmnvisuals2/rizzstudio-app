RizzStudio / Alternatives

An AI alternative to Lightkey, DMXIS & Beam

Most Mac lighting tools expect you to program the show yourself. RizzStudio programs it from your music.

There are great lighting tools for musicians on the Mac. Most of them, though, expect you to program the show: build scenes, sequence chases and place cues by hand. RizzStudio takes a different approach. It listens to your music and programs the show for you, then plays it from Ableton. Here's how it compares with tools people often look at.

Every tool below is good at what it does. This page explains where RizzStudio is different, so you can pick the right tool or combine them.

RizzStudio vs Lightkey

Lightkey is a polished Mac lighting controller with a strong effects engine and Ableton integration. You design the looks and cues yourself. RizzStudio is built around automatic, AI-generated programming from the audio of each song, and it exports the result into Ableton as MIDI so Ableton stays the clock.

RizzStudio vs DMXIS

DMXIS runs as a plugin inside your audio software, so lights are stored and automated alongside the music. RizzStudio also lives in your Ableton timeline (as DMX-over-MIDI clips), but the clips are generated for you from analysis of every kick, clap, drop and silence.

RizzStudio vs Beam for Live

Beam brings lighting control into Ableton with Max for Live devices you program and modulate yourself. RizzStudio is a standalone app that does the programming up front and writes the finished show back into your set, and it adds a 3D stage preview, fixture profiles and video rendering.

RizzStudio vs QLC+ and Captivate

QLC+ is a capable free, open-source lighting desk, and Captivate is a free generative DMX and visuals tool with Ableton Link. Both are great for live control. RizzStudio focuses on song-by-song show design, with section detection, numbered drops, pre-drop moments and instrument following, so the show is written for your exact tracks.

Use them together

Because RizzStudio outputs standard Art-Net and sACN, you can run it alongside an existing desk. Let RizzStudio handle the music-synced show and keep your controller for house lights and manual overrides.
